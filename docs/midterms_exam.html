reflection

The midterm examination was quite easy on the first and last parts of it. As they were just a multiple choice answer and a fill-in-the-blank of a java class script.

However, the second part wherein we were tasked to trace and note post and pre increments, and this was confusing and tiring as we had to tally each number up, and evaluate each answer.

Even so, I believe that the examination was simple yet challenging, and pushed me to think and evaluate much more carefully especially in syntaxing and incremental tracking.

Lastly, this experience leads me to practice more about classes and polymorphism, as learning should never stop and as I believe that these topics will be the next ones tackled in our class.
